Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This notice explains what HandyFlix Pro does with information on your device. HandyFlix Pro is designed as a local-first entertainment app: most of what you see in your library, history, and downloads stays on your phone or tablet.

1. Summary

  • No sign-in or HandyFlix Pro cloud account is required.
  • We do not operate a central server that stores your watchlist, history, or downloads.
  • Internet access is used to load catalogs, images, playback sources, and advertisements from third-party providers.
  • You control most data through in-app actions, Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising

HandyFlix Pro may show video advertisements before playback, before downloads, and during extended app use. Ad requests may be sent to third-party ad partners and may include standard technical data such as IP address, device type, app version, and ad interaction events required for measurement and billing. HandyFlix Pro does not sell a personal account profile because no account system is offered in this version.

3. Information stored on your device

HandyFlix Pro may store the following locally:

  • My List / saved titles
  • Watch history and playback progress
  • Search history
  • Selected seasons and watched-episode markers
  • Cached title details and artwork
  • Download queue, download status, and offline media files
  • App preferences such as playback and display settings

This information is kept in app storage on your device unless you delete it.

4. Information we do not collect through an account

Because there is no HandyFlix Pro account in this version, we do not collect your name, email address, password, or profile from inside the app itself.

If you choose Email support or Report a bug, your email app sends the message. You can edit the draft before sending. Bug-report templates may include device model, Android version, and app version to help troubleshooting.

5. Network activity

When you browse, search, open details, stream, download, or view advertisements, the app sends requests over the internet to retrieve catalogs, images, subtitles, media links, and ad media. Those requests may include standard technical data such as IP address, device type, timestamps, and request headers handled by third-party infrastructure.

HandyFlix Pro does not control how independent providers process that technical information. Their policies apply to those services.

6. Permissions

Depending on your device and the features you use, Android may ask for permissions such as:

  • Internet access — required for catalogs, streaming, downloads, and ads
  • Notifications — optional, for recommendations or download status
  • Storage / media access — used so downloads can be saved and managed on your device

You can review or revoke permissions in Android system settings.

7. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Android delivers them through system channels you can manage in Settings or in Android notification controls.

8. Your choices and controls

You can:

  • Remove items from My List, history, and downloads inside the app
  • Clear cached data by clearing app storage in Android settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove app-managed local records

Important: clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently delete local lists, progress, and app settings. Downloaded media files may remain on storage until you delete them manually.

9. Children's privacy

HandyFlix Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information through a child account because no account system is offered.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps within the app design to keep local data on your device and to use secure network connections where supported. No mobile app can guarantee absolute security against device compromise, malware, or physical access to an unlocked phone.

11. International users

Third-party services used for catalogs, playback, or advertising may process technical request data in locations outside your country. By using network features, you understand those providers may handle data under their own rules.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when app behavior or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
